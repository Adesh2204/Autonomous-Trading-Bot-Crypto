# What happens after the leverage configuration

The human has manually verified the registered team and approved API-wallet identities. That verification and the current request authorize only the deliberate all-ten 1x CROSS setup. They do not unlock entries, exits, cancellations, protective orders, transfers or account-mode changes. Keep `EXECUTION_MODE=PAPER`, `ALLOW_LIVE_ORDERS=false` and protection disabled.

## Verify the real configuration

The authoritative result is an exchange `activeAssetData` read for each of the ten `par:` markets, with the correct team address, `type=cross` and `value=1`. A prepared action, an SDK signature or an HTTP acknowledgement alone is insufficient. Confirm funded unified account, valid agent approval, healthy clock/budget, no positions and no ordinary or trigger orders again. Preserve the canonical signer nonce database and configuration journal.

## Keep collecting genuine PAPER data

Use current `par:` candles, books, mark, oracle and funding. Preserve 300 completed bars, 0.40% scanner ATR floor, 15 bps spread and Top-3. A fresh mark is not a substitute for a fresh completed candle. The captured venue responses contain stale/sparse candle histories; do not invent bars or substitute hourly reference CSVs. The complete segment may correctly return `NO_TRADE`.

Completed PAPER segments, healthy samples, rejected opportunities and downtime remain evidence even when there are no fills. They do not establish a profitable strategy, actual accounting, successful funded protection or readiness for unattended practice.

The infrastructure-only configuration-pacing change produces a new overall source fingerprint. Preserve the prior PAPER ledger and its recorded source identity. A later launch under the revised fingerprint must use a new separately named PAPER state directory unless a reviewed state migration explicitly preserves the checkpoint identity rules. Never rewrite saved source hashes or delete signer nonce history to bypass startup.

## Prepare one bounded LONG certification

Only after usable current data is demonstrated and the human separately authorizes a small maximum notional/risk budget, build a concrete controlled session from a genuine qualifying candidate. Do not size from the million-unit balance. Runtime minimum size, precision, liquidity and side-specific `availableToTrade` determine feasibility. If no qualifying setup exists, wait and record `NO_TRADE`.

Observe an actual exchange fill, positive position, actual fees, accepted reduce-only emergency protection for the filled size, then an authorized reduce-only SELL exit. Verify actual exit fills, fees/funding/closed P&L, zero position and no ordinary/trigger orders. An accepted order is not a fill. The configured protection is one entry ATR beyond the normal strategy stop with 0.5% execution allowance; that allowance does not guarantee a fill.

## SHORT, then funded recovery

Start SHORT only after LONG is fully flat and reconciled. Verify actual SELL entry, negative position, accepted protection and authorized reduce-only BUY exit, then flat/no leftovers and reconciled fees/P&L.

Within separately authorized bounds, certify cancellation, public/account reconnect, restart with authoritative existing-position/order recovery and duplicate prevention. Startup begins UNKNOWN. Resolve an ambiguous submission through exchange/account reads before considering a resend. Offline or PAPER scenarios keep their labels. Then consider supervised practice; do not start autonomous funded trading merely because leverage reads 1x.
