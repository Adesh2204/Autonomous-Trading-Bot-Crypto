# Existing exact-proposal approval mechanism

This is documentation of the tested workflow. No trade approval has been supplied, no required acknowledgment has been enabled and no execute command has been run.

1. The operator reads the fresh `proposal_review/proposal.json` and `report.md`, then explicitly approves its exact proposal hash, fixed quantity/entry limit, 25 USDPR opening-notional ceiling, 1x CROSS, one entry, two bounded exits, fixed exit floor and five signed actions maximum. Preparation/review instructions and an old approval cannot authorize this new proposal.
2. Required identity/attempt confirmations are `manually_verified_wallets`, `sole_signer_host_confirmed` and `maximum_one_entry`. The earlier human sole-host answer is recorded separately and does not authorize a trade. The displayed registered team account and API signer must be the approved identities.
3. Three separate risk acknowledgments are required: `unverified_stop_risk_accepted`, `qualification_spread_limit_accepted`, and `possible_manual_residual_recovery_accepted`. They mean accepting **no verified protective stop and no stop submission**, the isolated **50 bps** ceiling while production remains **15 bps**, and potentially unprotected residual exposure needing explicit operator recovery. Neither closing evidence nor approval certifies protective-stop acceptance. The 25 USDPR entry cap is not a guaranteed total-loss cap; fees/funding and unresolved exposure remain risks.
4. Only after an explicit trusted human reply may the authorization document record that actual reply as `operator_approval`, the exact `proposal_hash`, integer approval/expiry timestamps and confirmed acknowledgments. The generated `approval_REQUIRED_UNFILLED.json` is deliberately blank/false and must not be treated as permission or filled by assumption.
5. Approval must occur no more than 300,000 ms after the proposal's `observed_ms`. Its execution capability is valid for at most another 300,000 ms after actual approval. Every signed action must occur before that capability expires. Expiry, source/policy changes, used attempts or failed fresh checks block submission; no backdated approval or automatic substitution of a different proposal.
6. The existing launcher consumes the proposal, human authorization and source-bound full-regression receipt only through its separate `--execute` branch. The factory `build_qualification` in `algo-trading-bot/exchange/testnet_qualification.py` verifies the acknowledgments through `QualificationAuthorization` and `CertificationAuthorization`, current source/policy and frozen strategy, prior durable consumed/unresolved state, new flat-account reconciliation, current liquidity/limits/fees/margin/1x CROSS, and signer/account/nonce ownership. Only after those checks does it construct the actual SDK signer transport. No such execute branch or actual transport has been constructed in this review.
7. Global configuration remains `EXECUTION_MODE=PAPER`, `ALLOW_LIVE_ORDERS=false` and protection disabled. The human's exact authorization, if supplied, permits only this isolated bounded TESTNET_QUALIFICATION experiment, never production activation, repeated trading, a transfer or general account administration.

## Uncertain or residual exposure: exact recovery sequence

- Stop new entries. Preserve the one-entry-consumed marker, durable intent/client order ID, known exchange order ID and journal/checkpoint. An HTTP timeout consumes the attempted entry; it never earns a replacement entry.
- Use bounded read-only `orderStatus` lookups for the saved ID, the registered team's `userFills`, `frontendOpenOrders` and `clearinghouseState` for `dex=par`. Match reported fills, remaining quantity, side and fees to the saved intent. If reads fail or conflict, retain UNKNOWN/exposure truth and escalate; do not infer a fill or flat account.
- Only the active, still-authorized workflow may use its remaining bounded exit attempt after definite current position reconciliation. SELL only the actual LONG residual, reduce-only IOC, using fresh bid × 0.998 with runtime precision and never below the fixed approved floor. No top-up, oversizing, opposite position, uncertain duplicate, or new entry.
- If the workflow has halted/revoked authorization, its budget is exhausted, price is below the floor, permission expired, or status cannot be established, stop writes and show the exact known residual/unknown orders as EMERGENCY_EXIT_REQUIRED. Restart performs read-only recovery; it cannot reset the consumed entry or resume a revoked proposal. Manual intervention requires an explicit new recovery decision; do not silently extend price or time limits.
- After an authorized attempt, prove the entry through actual team-account fills matched to its exchange order and journal; reconcile actual fees/funding and exits. Verify flat from fresh exchange positions and open orders. Populate WHITELIST_TRADE_PROOF.json only with genuine IDs, quantities, prices/notional, timestamps and reconciliation. A verified fill does not imply a verified flat position or organizer whitelist confirmation.
